# Wholesale Net Revenue by Product Line, Month and Warehouse (PostgreSQL)

Net revenue after payment fees for the wholesale business of a ceramic-materials supplier, broken down by product line, month and warehouse, with warehouse shares, month-over-month trends and built-in sanity checks.

> **All data is synthetic**, generated by `src/generate_data.py` (fixed seed). Prices, volumes, warehouses and fees are invented.

## Objective

Management wants to understand wholesale revenue by product line and how it varies from month to month and between the three warehouses. Revenue has to be reported net of payment fees, and every payment method charges a different percentage of the order total.

## Techniques Used

- Conditional filtering and multi-column `GROUP BY` with `date_trunc` (months kept as dates so they sort chronologically)
- Unit-aware calculation of net revenue from a fee stored as a percentage
- Window functions on top of an aggregate view: `SUM() OVER (PARTITION BY ...)` for shares, `LAG()` for month-over-month change, `RANK()` for warehouse ranking
- A comparison query that quantifies the effect of a wrong formula
- SQL assertions (`DO` block with `ASSERT`) that fail the run if row counts, signs or reconciliations are off
- `NUMERIC` for money instead of floating point

## Key Findings

The table holds 2,100 orders from June to August 2026; 1,308 of them (62 %) are wholesale orders worth 4.79 million EUR gross, 91 % of all revenue.

- **Net revenue after fees is 4.75 million EUR**; payment fees cost 46,500 EUR (1.0 % of gross).
- **Credit cards are expensive even in wholesale.** They carry 21 % of the wholesale orders but 59 % of all fees (27,500 EUR), because the rate is 2.8 % against 0.5 % for bank transfers.
- **Digital inks are almost half of net revenue** (2.18 million EUR, 46 %), followed by ceramic pigments (22.7 %), ready-to-use glazes (16.4 %), frits (9.5 %) and engobes (5.4 %).
- **West is the largest warehouse (38.5 %)**, Central has 33.1 % and North 28.4 %. In July the West warehouse alone made 51 % of the digital-ink revenue.
- **August is 16 % below July** (1.40 against 1.66 million EUR net), with the sharpest single drop in digital inks at the North warehouse (-64 % month over month).
- **The unit-mixing shortcut hides the fees.** Computing net revenue as `SUM(total) - SUM(fee)` while the fee column holds percentages overstates net revenue by about 45,200 EUR, which is 97 % of the fees actually paid.

## Scripts

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `01_schema_and_load.sql` | Creates the `sales` table (money as `NUMERIC`) and loads the CSV |
| `02_net_revenue_wholesale.sql` | The main result: net revenue by product line, month and warehouse for wholesale orders (45 rows) |
| `03_formula_check.sql` | Compares the correct formula with the unit-mixing shortcut and shows fees by payment method |
| `04_warehouse_share_and_trend.sql` | Warehouse share within a product line and month, month-over-month change, rank |
| `05_sanity_checks.sql` | Assertions: 45 combinations, positive values, net below gross, gross minus fees equals net |
| `06_export_results.sql` | Writes the result views to `output/` |

## Calculation

```
net revenue = order total * (1 - payment_fee_pct / 100)
```

`payment_fee_pct` is the fee in percent of the order total (credit card 2.8, transfer 0.5, cash 1.0), so it must be turned into an amount before it is subtracted. Only orders with `client_type = 'Wholesale'` are included.

## Data

`data/sales.csv`, 2,100 rows.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `order_number` | Unique order number |
| `order_date` | Date of the order, 1 June to 31 August 2026 |
| `warehouse` | North, Central or West |
| `client_type` | Wholesale (tile producers, large distributors) or Retail (small studios) |
| `product_line` | Frits, Ready-to-use glazes, Engobes, Ceramic pigments, Digital inks |
| `quantity` | Number of packs |
| `unit_price_eur` | Price per pack |
| `total_eur` | `quantity * unit_price_eur` |
| `payment` | Credit card, Transfer or Cash |
| `payment_fee_pct` | Fee charged by the payment method, in percent of the total |

## How to Run

From the parent folder (`sql-portfolio`), start PostgreSQL once and run the scripts:

```bash
docker compose up -d --wait
for f in wholesale-net-revenue/sql/0*.sql; do
  docker compose exec -T -w /repo/wholesale-net-revenue db psql -U postgres -d glaze -v ON_ERROR_STOP=1 -f "sql/$(basename "$f")"
done
```

or all projects at once with `bash run_all.sh`. Results are written to `output/`. To regenerate the data: `python src/generate_data.py` (needs `numpy` and `pandas`).

## Limitations

- Three months of data are enough to show the mechanics but too few to separate seasonality from noise; the August drop is partly built into the synthetic demand curve.
- Fees are modelled as a flat percentage per payment method; real contracts may add fixed amounts or tiered rates.
- Returns, credit notes and currency effects are not part of the data.
